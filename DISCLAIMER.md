# Disclaimer and scope of use

oui-health is a tool for **interoperability reverse engineering**: making an Android phone you own
work fully with a Galaxy Watch you own, by understanding how Samsung's own companion software
decides what to enable.

**What this project does not do**

- It does not include, host, mirror, or redistribute any Samsung or Google software, in source or
  binary form. You supply any such binaries yourself, from a device or account you own, through
  official channels.
- It does not break, strip, or circumvent license enforcement, DRM, or paid entitlements. The goal
  is removing a *manufacturer* gate on *your own* hardware's basic functionality, not bypassing
  payment or anyone else's account.
- It does not help you impersonate another person's device or account, or interact with watches or
  data that are not yours.

**Your responsibility**

Reverse engineering for interoperability is lawful in many jurisdictions, but the details differ,
and a vendor's terms of service may say more than the law requires. You are responsible for
confirming that your use is lawful where you are and consistent with any agreement you accepted.
If your intended use is anything other than personal interoperability with hardware and accounts
you own, do not use this project until you have checked.

**No warranty.** Modifying how companion software runs can break the watch pairing, lose data, or
void support. Everything here is provided as-is, with no warranty. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).
